NOVA Ultimate v4 — Voice + Attachments + Microphone

Replace these files in GitHub:
- index.js
- public/index.html
- public/app.js
- public/styles.css

New microphone feature:
- 🎤 Mic uses the browser's speech-recognition microphone when supported.
- Tap Mic, speak, and the recognized text is inserted into the NOVA message box.
- Tap Stop to finish listening.
- The browser will ask for microphone permission when needed.

Also included from v3:
- NOVA / JARVIS / FRIDAY persona selector
- ElevenLabs Speak button
- Photo/file upload UI
- Camera capture and camera switching
- Attachment previews

Important: browser microphone speech recognition support varies by browser. Chrome/Chromium-based browsers generally provide the best support. The current backend receives the resulting text; it does not upload raw microphone audio.

NOVA 21 additions:
- Persistent server-side chat: data/nova-store/conversations.json (created automatically)
- Access/audit records: data/nova-store/access.json and audit.json
- Optional creator authorization: NOVA_CREATOR_TOKEN
- Optional read-only restricted access: NOVA_VIEWER_TOKEN
- Three ElevenLabs persona IDs: NOVA_VOICE_ID, JARVIS_VOICE_ID, FRIDAY_VOICE_ID
- Autonomous build progress is shown as human-readable stages without filenames or raw logs.

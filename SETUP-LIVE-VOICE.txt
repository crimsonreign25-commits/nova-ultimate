NOVA Ultimate — Live Voice Upgrade

This build adds hands-free live voice conversation.

Flow:
1. Tap Live Voice.
2. NOVA listens through the browser microphone.
3. Your speech is converted to text.
4. The text is sent to /assist with the selected persona.
5. NOVA speaks the response through /speak.
6. After speaking, NOVA listens again while Live Voice remains enabled.

Browser note:
- Microphone and audio playback permissions must be allowed.
- SpeechRecognition support depends on the browser/device.
- ElevenLabs must be configured on the backend for spoken replies.

No GitHub deployment is required yet. Test locally first.
